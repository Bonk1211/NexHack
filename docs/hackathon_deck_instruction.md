1. Introduction
NexHack 2026 is an AI hackathon designed to push participants, with 1 to 3 people as a team, to produce solutions that can survive beyond a one-day demo. Organised in partnership with Xenber Sdn. Bhd. as the sole sponsor and physical final host, the hackathon challenges participants to build AI systems for real enterprise operations and fintech trust problems. The competition is open to both university students and working adults in Malaysia, encouraging cross-functional teams that combine technical ability, product thinking, and business judgment.

The hackathon uses an online preliminary round to identify top 10 finalist teams, followed by a one-day physical final at Xenber Sdn. Bhd. where finalists present working prototypes, live demos, technical architecture & execution, commercial reasoning, and implementation roadmaps. The strongest projects may be considered for post-hackathon development, mentorship, pilot opportunities, or funding support, subject to Xenber’s assessment and mutually agreed terms and conditions.
2. Hackathon Philosophy: Build What the Market Will Pay For
The central philosophy of NexHack 2026 is practicality. The winning solutions should not merely look impressive; they should solve painful problems that real organizations, departments, fintech companies, or customers would realistically pay to adopt.
Technical depth matters: participants should demonstrate real system design, technical architecture & implementation, rational use of AI, and user experience.
 Business depth matters equally: participants should explain the target customers, pain point, pricing models, implementation roadmap, and commercialization potential.
 Depth beats breadth: judges should reward teams that solve one practical problem deeply rather than teams that build many shallow features.
Builder mindset matters: the best teams should think like product builders and entrepreneurs, not only coders.
3. Problem Statement
Main theme: Building Autonomous AI Workforce & Trusted Fintech Ecosystems
Organizations are under pressure to improve productivity, reduce operational risk, and respond faster to fraud, compliance, and customer threats. Participants must design and implement an AI-powered system that can assist, automate, or intelligently coordinate real business workflows while remaining explainable, controllable, and realistic for adoption.
The topic chosen is less important than the depth of execution. Judges reward teams that solve one practical problem deeply rather than teams that cover many features shallowly.

Participants need to propose a practical solution with their own creativity and innovation that is related to either track below.
Track 1: Agentic AI for Internal Enterprise Operations (HR, Finance, Compliance, IT Operations and so on)
Track 2: Fintech Risk & Fraud Intelligence (Fraud Detection, Anti-Money Laundering, Credit Risk, Scam Prevention and so on)

4. Target Participants and Team Structure
Open to the public, including students, fresh graduates, working adults, founders, engineers, designers and builders.
Team size: 1 to 3 participants per team.
Mixed teams are encouraged, especially teams that combine software engineering, AI/data, UX/product, domain knowledge, and business strategy.
